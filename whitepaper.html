Technical Framework & Regulatory Notice

Notice to the Global Community and Institutional Partners:

The ZSOZSO core ecosystem operates strictly as a fixed, decentralized ledger utility (100,000,000,000 tokens) designed to interface with international banking architectures, academic research networks, and planetary climate-cooling initiatives.

To ensure absolute adherence to evolving global regulations, including the European Union’s Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) framework, the public consumer-facing documentation is undergoing a long-term administrative review. Aligning state-level banking bureaucracy with cross-border legal systems is an extended institutional process.

Mandatory Regulatory Disclosure:
The information on this public landing page does not constitute an active offer, solicitation, or invitation to purchase crypto-assets within the European Economic Area. This project has not been approved or reviewed by any centralized competent authority. We are a decentralized and competent P2P authority.

Open-Source Architecture:
The technical engineering, architectural blueprints, and core targets of this decentralized network are fully open-source. Verified developers, sovereign bank systems, and academic researchers can independently audit the active source code directly through our official repository channels.

For early versions of the whitepapers and live code-level developments, please reference our repository trees or track foundational updates via ifinta on GitHub or through our official social channels. Until that time, only this notice stands as the legally binding statement from the issuer. Any other legacy text remaining on this platform is preserved strictly for historical reasons to document our initial commitments to our community. The implementation of MiCA is a sound regulatory decision that has prompted deep analysis within our team, and we look forward to publishing our formal responses in due course.
